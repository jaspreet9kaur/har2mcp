# Community conduct

Be respectful, discuss behavior and code constructively, and protect others' privacy. Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted contact and publishing personal information are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation.

A private conduct-reporting contact and an independent alternate contact are not configured yet. They must be established before organized community onboarding. Do not post confidential complaints in public issues. The project's vulnerability-reporting form is for security findings, not a substitute for conduct reporting.
